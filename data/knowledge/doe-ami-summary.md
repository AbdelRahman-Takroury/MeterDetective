# Advanced metering infrastructure context

Advanced metering infrastructure combines interval meters, communications, utility data systems, and operational processes. A consumption anomaly can originate in measurement, communications, customer load, distributed generation, or upstream grid conditions. Investigation therefore benefits from meter history, peer comparisons, data-quality checks, and network context.

Remote and field actions should be governed by utility procedures. MeterDetective treats recommendations as decision support and simulates actions only after an approval record exists. It does not infer fraud from an anomalous load pattern.

This is a short MeterDetective-authored summary, not a quotation or substitute for the linked U.S. Department of Energy material.
