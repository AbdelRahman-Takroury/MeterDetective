# Operational evidence and smart-grid investigations

Smart-grid investigations should preserve provenance, timestamps, system boundaries, and the distinction between observed evidence and analyst inference. A meter reading by itself is not enough to identify a cause. Related communications, upstream assets, configuration, and operational history may be relevant.

Security and operational events should be auditable. Records should support reconstruction of what happened, which systems and assets were affected, and what actions were authorized. Controls should be proportionate to risk, and higher-impact actions should require explicit authorization.

This is a short MeterDetective-authored operational summary, not a quotation or substitute for NISTIR 7628 Rev. 1. Consult the linked NIST publication for authoritative guidance.
